# Highway One — roadmap for future sessions

Graphics is the current focus. Everything below is queued for later sessions, roughly in priority order.
Each item is scoped so it fits inside one ~3 h block.

## Free roam, GTA-style
1. **Pedestrians** — instanced walkers on sidewalks, the boardwalk and the pier; crosswalk crossings that traffic (and the rules monitor) respects; sunbathers and joggers on the beach.
2. **Police** — a patrol car in traffic (black-and-white sedan, light bar). Speeding or running a red near it starts a pull-over: siren, "PULL OVER" prompt, stop within 10 s to pay a fine, or flee and lose the score/money. Escalates only in Driving Test mode; free roam just fines.
3. **Gas station** — a Chevron-style canopy on 2nd St; the fuel gauge already drains. Stop at a pump and hold a key to refuel (cost from earnings). Empty tank = engine dies until pushed/reset.
4. **Parking** — marked bays in the pier lot, beach lot and the new block parking lots; park fully inside the lines for a bonus; parallel-parking challenge on Main St.
5. **Car damage** — cracked-windshield overlay, dented hood mesh, rattles and a warning lamp after hard hits; repair at the 4th St garage.
6. **Phone / map** — full-screen map (the nav master canvas) with fast travel to POIs, mission list, earnings, collectibles (viewpoints on Canyon Rd, hidden beach coves, pier photo ops).
7. **Ambient life** — seagull flocks, a beach bus and an ice-cream truck on fixed routes, sailboats and a fishing boat offshore, a lifeguard truck on the sand, dogs on the boardwalk.
8. **Traffic upgrades** — lane changes, horns when blocked, headlights in tunnels, a few extra body types (pickup, van, bus).
9. **Radio** — song titles read by the DJ, more music variety, news and traffic that react to the weather and the time of day.
10. **Photo mode** — free camera (the `debugShot` hook already exists) with a hood/chase view for screenshots only.
